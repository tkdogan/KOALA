Overview

ISO 10211 (Thermal bridges in building construction — Heat flows and surface temperatures), Annex C, defines four test reference cases. A program counts as a two-dimensional steady-state high-precision method if it reproduces cases 1 and 2, and as a three-dimensional one if it reproduces all four.

KOALA runs these cases as automated tests on every build: cases 1 and 2 with the 2D solver of the standalone app, and all four with the 3D solver used by the Rhino plugin. Each case is solved at the cell size KOALA recommends for the geometry (the Auto setting), not a hand-tuned grid. The ISO grid rule is checked as well: solving with twice the cell size may change the heat flow by less than 1 %. The tables on this page are generated directly from those test results.

ISO 10211 Test Case 1

Description: Half a square column, 1.0 m wide and 2.0 m high (λ = 1 W/(m·K)), with prescribed surface temperatures: top 20 °C, left and bottom 0 °C, right side adiabatic.

Criterion: The temperatures at 28 equidistant points may differ from the analytical solution by at most 0.1 °C. KOALA compares against the exact series solution, which ISO lists rounded to 0.1 °C.

ISO 10211 Test Case 2

Description: A frame-like section of concrete, wood and insulation with a 1.5 mm aluminium strip (λ = 230 W/(m·K)) next to insulation (λ = 0.029 W/(m·K)), a conductivity ratio of almost 8000. Exterior 0 °C with Rse = 0.06 m²K/W, interior 20 °C with Rsi = 0.11 m²K/W.

Criterion: Temperatures at points A–I within 0.1 °C, total heat flow within 0.1 W/m of 9.5 W/m. KOALA's graded mesh puts a grid line on every material interface, so the 1.5 mm strip is resolved exactly even with 2.5 mm cells.

ISO 10211 Test Case 3

Description: Two rooms on top of each other in the corner of two external walls (100 mm λ 1.0 | 50 mm insulation λ 0.04 | 150 mm λ 0.7). The 150 mm floor slab (λ 2.5) runs through one wall and projects 600 mm outside like a balcony, with a 50 mm floor layer (λ 1.0) in the upper room. Room α (below) is at 20 °C, room β (above) at 15 °C, both with Rsi = 0.20 m²K/W; outside γ is 0 °C with Rse = 0.05 m²K/W.

Criterion: The heat flows from each environment within 1 %, and the lowest surface temperature in each room (in the three-dimensional corners) within 0.1 °C.

ISO 10211 Test Case 4

Description: A point thermal bridge: an iron bar of 100 × 50 mm (λ = 50 W/(m·K)) penetrates a 200 mm insulation layer (λ = 0.1 W/(m·K)) of 1 × 1 m and projects 400 mm into the interior. Interior 1 °C, exterior 0 °C, both surfaces with R = 0.10 m²K/W.

Criterion: The heat flow within 1 % of 0.540 W, and the highest temperature on the external surface within 0.005 °C of 0.805 °C.
